About Ann

Groomer owned. Cat specialist. Still on Wilkins Court.

Ann is a Maryland state-certified groomer. Pets have been the constant in her life, from Bassetts to Yorkies, and the shop was built around that. She has lived and traveled widely, and she still works the table herself.

The shop grooms household pets and show animals. The standard she publishes is simple: quality work, and service that matters as much as the haircut. A visit is also a chance to talk through coat, habits, and what to watch at home.
